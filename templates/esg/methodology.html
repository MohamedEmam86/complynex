{{ _('Why a fourth "Regulatory" pillar') }}

{{ _('Global ESG frameworks (GRI, IFRS S1/S2, TCFD) are built for listed multinationals and don\'t weight CBE/FRA supervisory standing at all. For an Egyptian PSP, AML/CFT programme maturity, licensing standing, and regulatory reporting track record are as material to stakeholders as the classic E/S/G pillars — so I-ESG Egypt gives them their own 15%% weight rather than burying them inside Governance.') }}
